\subsection{Overview}\label{overview}

Phase 2a. Extracts the adult ICU cohort from MIMIC-IV and applies all three pre-specified Sepsis-3 onset label variants (Cohen et al., 2024).

\textbf{Outputs} (per variant): \texttt{cohort\_A.parquet}, \texttt{cohort\_B.parquet}, \texttt{cohort\_C.parquet}, \texttt{label\_variance\_table.csv} (M1 deliverable).

\subsection{1. Base Tables}\label{base-tables}

Load patients, admissions, and ICU stays from MIMIC-IV.

\subsection{2. Base Cohort Criteria}\label{base-cohort-criteria}

Restrict to adults (≥18 years), first ICU stay per patient, minimum LOS ≥ 4 hours.

\begin{verbatim}
## Base cohort: 65,078 stays, 65,078 unique patients
\end{verbatim}

\subsection{3. Suspected Infection}\label{suspected-infection}

Suspected infection = antibiotic + culture close in time. Generates all ABX--culture pairs within each \texttt{hadm\_id}, then filters per-variant below.

\begin{verbatim}
## Antibiotic orders: 820,798
\end{verbatim}

\begin{verbatim}
## Culture samples: 1,530,517
\end{verbatim}

\begin{verbatim}
## ABX-culture pairs (raw): 5,897,222
\end{verbatim}

\subsection{4. SOFA Components}\label{sofa-components}

Load labs, chart events, and vasopressor infusions for SOFA delta computation.

\begin{verbatim}
## Pre-filtering large tables to 65078 cohort hadm_ids...
\end{verbatim}

\subsection{5. Vectorized SOFA Delta}\label{vectorized-sofa-delta}

Uses \texttt{foverlaps} to compute SOFA in the evaluation and baseline windows without any per-row loop.

\subsection{6. Build Cohort Per Variant}\label{build-cohort-per-variant}

Applies ABX--culture windows, SOFA delta check, and exclusion criteria for each of the three pre-registered label variants.

\begin{verbatim}
## Variant A: 65078 total stays, 6252 sepsis onsets (9.6%)
## Variant B: 65078 total stays, 6447 sepsis onsets (9.9%)
## Variant C: 65078 total stays, 12581 sepsis onsets (19.3%)
\end{verbatim}
