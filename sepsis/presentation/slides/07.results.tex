\begin{frame}{VI. Results: H3, the Empty Pre-Treatment Window}
\vspace*{0pt}%
{\footnotesize Status: \notevaluable, because its contrast cannot be formed.\par}
\begin{block}{Proposition (proved in the thesis)}
  \footnotesize In Variants A, B, C and E, onset is assigned to the
  \emph{later} event of the antibiotic--culture pair, so onset can never
  precede the treatment anchor. The pre-treatment window therefore contains no
  onsets \textbf{by construction}, not by observation.
\end{block}

\begin{block}{H2: coefficient instability (descriptive)}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep1pt\scriptsize
  \item \pcHTwoEst{} of \pcHTwoNTerms{} covariates unstable across labels:
        \pcHTwoNSign{} by sign, \pcHTwoNMag{} by magnitude.
  \item \textbf{Only \pcHTwoNRobust{} remain unstable after accounting for

        is a loose upper bound.
  \item \textbf{Ablation} (post-hoc): dropping the \pcAblNDropped{}
        action-derived covariates costs \pcAblDeltaAurocPrimaryB{} AUROC
        (primary, B), while instability falls to \pcAblHTwoNUnstable{} of
        \pcAblHTwoNTerms. The AUROC reduction is small but distinguishable from zero.
\end{itemize}
\end{block}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.49\textwidth}
\begin{block}{Equity by label (exploratory)}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep1pt\scriptsize
  \item Subgroup AUROC: \pcEquityNContrastsEOne{} contrasts clear the
        100-event floor and \textbf{none survives} BH correction.
  \item Only two race categories meet the 100-event floor, one of which is
        \emph{Unknown}, so no powered comparison between two recorded
        categories is possible.
  \item Label sensitivity \emph{does} survive: Unknown
        $+\pcLabelSensUnknownPp$\,pp ($q = \pcLabelSensUnknownQ$), Unable to
        obtain $+\pcLabelSensUnablePp$\,pp ($q = \pcLabelSensUnableQ$).
  \item Both indicate missing demographic information, so the result is read as
        an association with \textbf{documentation completeness}, not as a group
        difference~\cite{wang2022equity}.
\end{itemize}
\end{block}
\end{column}
\end{atucols}
