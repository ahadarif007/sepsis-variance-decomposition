\begin{frame}{VI. Results: Why H3 Was Not Evaluable}
\vspace*{0pt}%
{\footnotesize Status: \notevaluable\par}
\begin{block}{Structural reason}
  \footnotesize Onset is the \emph{later} event of the antibiotic--culture
  pair, so it cannot precede treatment: the window is empty \textbf{by
  definition}.
\end{block}

\vspace{2pt}
\begin{center}\tablestyle\footnotesize

F & Sepsis-3 Early-Onset & \pcPreTreatPersonHoursVarF & \textbf{\pcPreTreatOnsetsVarF}
  & \pcPreTreatAurocPrimaryF{} / \pcPreTreatAurocGbtF \\
D & Deterioration$^{*}$ & \pcPreTreatPersonHoursVarD & \textbf{\pcPreTreatOnsetsVarD}
  & \pcPreTreatAurocPrimaryD{} / \pcPreTreatAurocGbtD \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{itemize}\itemsep1pt\footnotesize
  \item \textbf{F} (post-hoc): standard onset $\min(t_{\text{susp}},
        t_{\text{SOFA}})$ moves \pcFNMovedEarlier{} of \pcFNOnsets{} onsets
        earlier, \pcFNBeforeAnchorTest{} into the test window.
\end{itemize}
{\tiny $^{*}$D's SOFA components overlap the model inputs, so its AUROC is not
an independent result.\par}

\note{\small
\begin{itemize}
  \item The pre-registered onset rule produced no pre-treatment onsets, so
        the contrast could not be formed.
  \item This holds in Variants A, B, C and E: onset is assigned to the later
        event of the pair, so the window is empty by definition, not because
        no events were observed.
  \item Variant F keeps B's cases; median onset shift \pcFMedianShiftH\,h.
  \item The concern about treatment-dependent case membership remains
        relevant; its effect on onset timing depends on the onset rule.
  \item These AUROCs assess detection at the assigned onset, not advance
        prediction.
\end{itemize}}
\end{frame}
