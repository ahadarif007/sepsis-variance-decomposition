\begin{frame}{VII. Discussion: Main Findings and Limitations}
\begin{atucols}[t]
\begin{column}{0.50\textwidth}
\textbf{Main findings}
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep3pt\footnotesize
  \item \textbf{Onset timing:} H3 not evaluable; standard timing moves
        \pcFNMovedEarlier{} onsets earlier.
  \item \textbf{Definition:} prevalence \pcPctOnsetsA--\pcPctOnsetsC\%,
        A--B~$\kappa = \pcKappaVsBA$, yet AUROC range only \pcSpreadLabel.
  \item \textbf{Utility:} \pcUtilityOptBurdenGbtB{} false alerts per true
        alert at best (benchmark 1.4).
\end{enumerate}
\vspace{2pt}
\begin{alertblock}{}
  \footnotesize Improving discrimination alone cannot resolve limitations
  introduced by outcome membership, onset timing and alert burden.
\end{alertblock}
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}
\textbf{Limitations}
\begin{enumerate}\itemsep3pt\footnotesize
  \item Current-hour onset detection, not advance warning.
  \item A--C share one treatment anchor, so part of their spread is
        definitional.
  \item H6 is non-inferiority against one untuned GBT.
\end{enumerate}
\vspace{2pt}
{\scriptsize\color{MidGrey} Also: single centre; eICU
\pcExtMicroCoverage\% culture coverage (\pcExtNEventsB{} events); post-hoc
variants D and F.\par}
\end{column}
\end{atucols}

\note{\small
\begin{itemize}
  \item Finding 1: treatment-dependent membership and onset timing. The
        pre-registered onset rule made H3 non-evaluable.
  \item Finding 3: the best Variant B operating point.
  \item The three limitations shown are the ones that bound the central
        claims; the rest qualify the scope of particular analyses.
  \item Cohort: first ICU stays of at least 4\,h.
  \item The pre-registered onset rule differs from the standard rule;
        Variant F restores standard timing post hoc.
  \item Variant D is an unvalidated label whose components overlap the model
        inputs.
  \item Post-hoc analyses are labelled; the literature review is narrative.
\end{itemize}}
\end{frame}
