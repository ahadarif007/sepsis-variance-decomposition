\note{\small
\begin{itemize}
  \item Suspected infection is an antibiotic paired with a culture; SOFA$-$
        and SOFA$+$ are the hours before and after suspected infection in
        which a $\geq 2$-point SOFA increase is accepted.
  \item A and C are pre-specified alternatives to the primary definition B.
  \item Onset is assigned at suspected infection, $t_{\text{susp}}$. SOFA
        determines case membership, not onset time.
  \item A and B have similar prevalence but low case agreement.
\end{itemize}}
