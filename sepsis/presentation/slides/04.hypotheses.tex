\begin{frame}{III. Research Question and Pre-Registered Hypotheses}
\begin{block}{Research Question}
  \small How much of the variation in reported performance comes from the
  model, and how much from label, split, timing and metric?
\end{block}

\vspace{2pt}
\begin{center}\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.12}\small
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
\textbf{H} & \textbf{Name} & \textbf{Pre-registered claim} \\
\midrule
H1 & Label dominance         & label spread $\geq$ model spread (AUROC) \\
H2 & Coefficient instability & $\geq 1$ sign flip or $\geq 2$-fold change \\
H3 & Treatment leakage       & pre-treatment AUROC lower by $\geq 0.03$ \\
H4 & Split optimism          & temporal AUROC $\geq 0.02$ below random \\
H5 & Metric divergence       & utility falls more than AUROC externally \\
H6 & Model-class null        & GBT $-$ primary AUROC $\leq 0.02$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

{\scriptsize Holm correction across H1--H6.\quad Estimand: current-hour onset
detection, not advance prediction.\par}

\note{\small
\begin{itemize}
  \item Setting: real-time sepsis detection in MIMIC-IV.
  \item Full question: how much variation comes from the model, and how much
        from how sepsis is defined, how data are split, when prediction is
        made relative to treatment, and which metric is reported.
  \item H2 and H3 yield no p-value. The reported H5 analysis uses a post-hoc
        substitute estimand.
\end{itemize}}
\end{frame}
