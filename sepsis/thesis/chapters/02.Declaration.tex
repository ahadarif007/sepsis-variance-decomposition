\section*{Use of Generative AI}

During this project I applied the PAIR framework to use generative AI
responsibly.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Problem:} building a complete pipeline for real-time sepsis
    onset prediction required cohort extraction and labelling under several
    Sepsis-3 readings, person-hour panel construction, model implementation
    across five model classes, an external-validation arm, a clinical-metrics
    and inference layer, and the documentation of all of it.

    \item \textbf{AI:} I used Claude to discuss implementation choices, to
    refactor early prototypes into the twelve-stage structure presented here,
    to audit that code for defects, and to produce documentation drafts.

    \item \textbf{Interaction:} all generated output was critically reviewed,
    modified and adapted to fit this study's design. The research question, the
    pre-registered hypotheses, the label definitions, the model choices, the
    configuration values, the decisions recorded as Protocol Amendments 1--8
    and the reported results are my own.

    \item \textbf{Reflection:} this approach improved development speed and
    documentation quality while keeping the experimental design, the analysis
    and the final conclusions my own work.
\end{itemize}

Generative AI was \emph{not} used to generate data, to produce or alter any
result, or to decide what the results mean. Every quantity this study estimates
(every AUROC, interval, p-value, count, rate and calibration statistic) is
written into this document mechanically from the pipeline's own output tables
by \texttt{thesis\_constants.R} (Appendix~\ref{app:repro}), and a quantity the
pipeline did not produce typesets as a visible marker rather than as a
plausible number. Three classes of number are typed rather than generated, and
none of them is a result of this study: figures quoted from prior work, each
carrying its citation; thresholds and design constants fixed by the
pre-registration before any modelling, such as the equivalence margin and the
correction levels; and, in running prose, values rounded for readability from
the generated macro that states them exactly in the adjacent table. The
analytical claims, the hypothesis verdicts and the limitations are mine, and I
take full responsibility for the content of this thesis.
