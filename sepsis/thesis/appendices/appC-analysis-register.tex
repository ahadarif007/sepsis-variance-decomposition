\chapter{Analysis Register}
\label{sec:register}

Table~\ref{tab:register} records the status of every analysis reported in
Chapter~\ref{ch:evaluation} and the multiplicity correction applied to it. Its confirmatory and
exploratory rows are transcribed from
\texttt{12\_analysis\_\allowbreak register\allowbreak .parquet}, which the
inference stage writes from the same objects it corrects. Two rows marked
$^\P$ have no counterpart in that file, because they are scored in stages 07
and 08 and enter no corrected family; they are included so that the
exploratory record is complete.
