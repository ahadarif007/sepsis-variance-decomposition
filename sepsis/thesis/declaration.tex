I declare that this thesis is my own work, that it has not been submitted for
any other academic award, and that all sources consulted are acknowledged in
the text and listed in the references. The research question, the
pre-registered hypotheses, the study design and the interpretation of the
findings are my own, as are the decisions recorded as Protocol Amendments 1--7
and the judgements about what the evidence does and does not support.

\section*{Use of Generative AI}

In accordance with ATU's Academic Integrity Policy, I declare the following use
of generative AI. An AI coding assistant (Anthropic's Claude, accessed through
Claude Code) was used throughout the development of this project, and the
repository accompanying this thesis records that use rather than concealing it.
Specifically, it was used to:

\begin{itemize}
    \item implement, refactor and debug the R and Python pipeline in
    \texttt{sepsis/\allowbreak project/}, working from specifications and
    design decisions I set;

    \item audit that pipeline for defects, including several that materially
    changed the results reported here and that are documented in
    \texttt{sepsis/feedback/};

    \item draft and revise prose in this document, which I then edited,
    verified against the pipeline output, and accepted or rejected line by
    line;

    \item prepare the internal development record in
    \texttt{sepsis/GEN-AI/} and \texttt{sepsis/feedback/}, which are working
    documents for the project rather than part of this submission.
\end{itemize}

It was \emph{not} used to generate data, to produce or alter any result, or to
decide what the results mean. Every quantity this study itself estimates ---
every AUROC, interval, p-value, count, rate and calibration statistic --- is
written into the document mechanically from the pipeline's own output tables by
\texttt{thesis\_constants.R} (Appendix~\ref{app:repro}), and a quantity the
pipeline did not produce typesets as a visible marker rather than as a
plausible number. Three classes of number are typed rather than generated, and
none of them is a result of this study: figures quoted from prior work, each
carrying its citation; thresholds and design constants fixed by the
pre-registration before any modelling, such as the equivalence margin and the
correction levels; and, in running prose, values rounded for readability from
the generated macro that states them exactly in the adjacent table. The
analytical claims, the hypothesis verdicts and the limitations are mine, and I
take full responsibility for the content of this thesis.
